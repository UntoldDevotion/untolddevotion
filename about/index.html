About Us

Made with devotion.

Untold Devotion Interactive was built on a simple idea: the best digital experiences are the ones made with genuine care.

We are Lee and Kerry — two creators with different instincts, different strengths, and one shared ambition: to build games and apps that people actually want to spend time with.

Lee

Drawn to puzzles, logic, challenge and clever mechanics — the kind of experiences that make you stop, think, experiment and come back for one more attempt.

Kerry

Drawn to progression, discovery and worlds that grow — experiences where every step feels meaningful, where environments evolve, stories unfold and the player can see the impact of what they have achieved.

Those two sides are at the heart of Untold Devotion Interactive. One side challenges the mind. The other rewards the journey.

Together, they give us the freedom to create everything from quick, addictive puzzle games to deeper progression adventures, alongside useful and meaningful digital apps.

Why we build what we build

We are not interested in creating things simply because an algorithm says they might perform well. We want every project to have a reason to exist.

That means memorable mechanics instead of empty repetition. Progress that feels earned. Worlds with personality. Apps that solve genuine problems. And experiences designed around the person using them — not just the numbers they generate.

We are independent creators, which means our ideas can start small, change direction, become strange, ambitious, personal or completely unexpected. There is no boardroom deciding what Untold Devotion should become. Just two people building things we believe are worth making.

The name

The name Untold Devotion reflects exactly that. Behind every finished game, every world, every puzzle and every app are the countless unseen hours of designing, testing, rebuilding and obsessing over details that most people will never know were there.

That devotion is the part that often goes untold. We want it to be felt in everything we create.

Original games. Meaningful apps.
Experiences made with devotion.
