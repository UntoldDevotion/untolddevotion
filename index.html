The Studio

What we stand for

01

A reason to exist

We don't build things because an algorithm says a category might perform well. Every project has to justify itself — memorable mechanics instead of empty repetition, progress that feels earned.

02

Made by hand

Every level is designed deliberately, not generated. If a puzzle is in the game, someone sat down and decided exactly how it should work — and how it should mislead you.

03

Straight with you

Where a game is ad-supported we say so plainly, ask before any ad loads, and let you change that choice whenever you like. What we collect is set out in full in our privacy policy.

About Us

Two people, two instincts

Untold Devotion Interactive was built on a simple idea: the best digital experiences are the ones made with genuine care. We're Lee and Kerry — two creators with different instincts, different strengths, and one shared ambition.

Lee

Drawn to puzzles, logic, challenge and clever mechanics — the kind of experiences that make you stop, think, experiment and come back for one more attempt.

Kerry

Drawn to progression, discovery and worlds that grow — where every step feels meaningful, environments evolve, stories unfold and you can see the impact of what you've achieved.
