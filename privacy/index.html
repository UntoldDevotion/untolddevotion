Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy covers the websites and mobile games published by Untold Devotion Interactive (“we”, “us”), including Trap Tap: 100 Levels of Lies.

The short version

Our games collect no personal data, contain no advertising, include no analytics or tracking software, and share nothing with third parties. There is no account to create and nothing to opt out of.

Information our games collect

None. Our games do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Location data of any kind
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage, gameplay, or crash analytics
  • Photos, contacts, files, or any other content on your device

Data stored on your device

Game progress and high scores are saved locally on your own device using standard operating-system storage. This information never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Third-party services

Our games contain no third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or social media SDKs.

Our games are distributed through the Google Play Store. Google collects information about downloads, purchases and crashes under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for detail on how Google handles that information.

This website

This website is static and sets no tracking cookies. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; Cloudflare processes standard server request logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and delivery purposes under its own privacy policy. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to serve the font files.

Children's privacy

Our games are rated suitable for ages 3 and over and are designed to be safe for children. Because we collect no data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction).

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and equivalent legislation you have rights to access, correct, or erase personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data about our players, there is nothing for us to retrieve, amend, or delete. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will investigate.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will update this policy and note the change here before that feature ships. The “last updated” date above always reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@untolddevotion.com.

Note for the site owner: this policy reflects the “no data collected” declaration on your Play Store listing. If you later add analytics, crash reporting, ads, or an SDK that phones home, this page and your Play Data Safety form must both be updated to match — a mismatch is a common cause of Play policy enforcement. Delete this note before publishing.
